I am **Jiaqing (Vincent) Hu**, a visual computing researcher with an **M.Sc. in Visual Computing from Simon Fraser University**, awarded in May 2026. My interests lie at the intersection of computer animation, computer vision, and machine learning, particularly efficient motion generation and the evaluation of learned animation systems.

I co-authored [F3AMD: Fast FiLM-Conditioned Fourier Autoregressive Motion Diffusion](/blog/f3amd), published in _Computer Graphics Forum_ for **SCA 2026**. The work explores how architectural and sampling choices can make diffusion-based character animation fast enough to scale to large crowds. My contributions included extensive ablation studies, refactoring code for the new framework, independently testing architectural ideas, and reporting experimental findings in close collaboration with the team. My broader projects include learned motion matching, reinforcement learning for character control, and visual understanding of images and video.
